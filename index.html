Day 1

Pass the campaign into Adapty

After a one-time SDK setup, the YouTube or Meta campaign each install came from arrives as a user attribute and picks the flow at first open. No app release.

Week 1

One flow per promise

Form, military calisthenics and the plan each get their own first screens; installs with no campaign keep today’s first launch.

Week 2

Judge each ad on what its installs earn

Each flow against today’s, measured on trials, paid subscriptions and what each install from that ad earns, so spend follows the promise that pays.

All three phones run the whole flow with the five ideas. The YouTube and Meta ads we opened link to the App Store, Google Play or profit-app-link.com. The only Apple Ads search we see for your account in the US is “вода” (Russian for “water”), at a 5% share of voice: a search for your Water Reminder app, most likely, not ProFit, so no phone here starts from Apple Ads.
